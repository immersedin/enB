What a handshake proves

Four layers, one job each. No server.

StepMovesProves
/invite Signed blob, timed. Id authentic while it lives.
DHT Owner-signed Kademlia records. Address, no directory.
Noise XX X25519+ChaChaPoly handshake. Both ends prove keys.
Double Ratchet Fresh X25519 DH per message. Forward secrecy.
SQLCipher Encrypted SQLite. Nothing readable on disk.

The interface is a prompt

One line; commands do the rest.

/invite [ttl]

Signs an invite.

/connect <code|id|file>

Resolves, verifies, connects.

/chat <name>

Opens a chat.

/peerid

Copies your id.

/copyid <name>

Copies a contact id.

/dht

Nodes, records, endpoint.

/bootstrap <host:port>

Adds a DHT node.
