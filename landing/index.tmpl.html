A level for where you are

Reported events in, one index out, for you and every city you track.

  1. strike41.8 kmNEongoing
  2. shelling92.4 kmEended
  3. strike118 kmNNWended
  4. ground156 kmWSWended
  5. shelling204 kmSSEended
  6. strike260 kmNEended
  7. other310 kmNWended

Distances and bearings are illustrative; the app computes them from your position and each report.

LevelIndexMeans
L1 80-100 Immediate danger close by.
L2 60-79 Serious activity nearby.
L3 35-59 Events within tens of km.
L4 10-34 Distant or stale reports.
L5 0-9 No threat in the data (not a guarantee).

Every reported event (shelling, strike, ground action) scores by distance and age, weighted by confirmations, and a noisy-OR keeps one confirmed report from being averaged away. /danger ai asks an LLM (Pollinations by default, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint) to weigh the same events plus Google News headlines. Each tracked city gets its own level on the Main tab; levels 1 to 3 ring an alarm you can silence.

What a handshake proves

Four layers, one job each. No server.

StepMovesProves
/invite Signed blob, timed. Id authentic while it lives.
DHT Owner-signed Kademlia records. Address, no directory.
Noise XX X25519+ChaChaPoly handshake. Both ends prove keys.
Double Ratchet Fresh X25519 DH per message. Forward secrecy.
SQLCipher Encrypted SQLite. Nothing readable on disk.

The interface is a prompt

One line; commands do the rest.

/danger [ai]

Local score; ai asks an LLM.

/event add <lat> <lon>

Reports a combat event.

/events

Stored events with distances.

/city add <query>

Tracks a city’s danger level.

/city search <query>

Finds settlements to track.

/sound

Per-level alert sounds.

/invite [ttl]

Signs an invite.

/connect <code|id|file>

Resolves, verifies, connects.

/chat <name>

Opens an E2EE chat.

/peerid

Copies your peer id.

/dht

Nodes, records, endpoint.

/bootstrap <host:port>

Adds a DHT node.

/update

Installs the latest release.
